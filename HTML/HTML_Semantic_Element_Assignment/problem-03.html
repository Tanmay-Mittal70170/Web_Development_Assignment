Intro to AIIMS, DELHI

Welcome to the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi — one of India's leading institutions for medical education, healthcare, and biomedical research.

Established in 1956, AIIMS New Delhi has played a significant role in advancing medical education, patient care, and medical research in India. The institute provides comprehensive healthcare services through its specialized departments and is known for its highly qualified medical professionals, researchers, and students.

Our website provides information about medical departments, doctors, academic programs, research, hospital services, admissions, and important announcements.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality patient care, promote excellence in medical education, and advance healthcare through innovative research and technology.

Patient Appointment Registration

Welcome to the AIIMS Delhi Patient Appointment Registration Portal. This form is designed to help patients register their details and request an appointment with the appropriate department or specialist at AIIMS Delhi.

By filling out this form, patients can provide essential information such as their personal details, contact information, preferred department, medical concerns, and appointment preferences. This helps streamline the appointment process and reduces the time required for registration.

    

Benifits Of This Form:

  • Easy Registration: Register for an appointment from anywhere without visiting the hospital registration counter first.
  • Saves Time: Reduces waiting time and makes the registration process faster and more convenient.
  • Better Organization: Patient information and appointment preferences can be collected in an organized manner.
  • Department Selection: Patients can select the department relevant to their healthcare needs.
  • Convenient Access: The form can be completed using a computer, tablet, or mobile device.
  • Improved Patient Experience: A simple registration process helps make hospital visits more convenient and organized.

Please enter all required information carefully and accurately before submitting the form.
