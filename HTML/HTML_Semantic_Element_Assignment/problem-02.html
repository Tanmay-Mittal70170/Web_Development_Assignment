Company Recruiting For:

We are looking for talented and passionate engineers to join our team and contribute to the development of next-generation AI, software, VR, and hardware technologies.

Looking For:

1.Software Engineer:

We are looking for a Software Engineer with strong programming skills and a good understanding of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. The candidate will work on developing intelligent software solutions, AI-powered features, and innovative applications.

  

2. Robotics Engineer(VR and hardware):

We are looking for a Robotics Engineer interested in robotics, VR systems, embedded systems, sensors, electronics, and hardware development. The candidate will work on our VR devices and other hardware technologies, helping design, develop, and improve real-world systems.



Eligiblity For the Job:

1.Software Engineer

  • Education: B.Tech/B.E./M.Tech/MCA in Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, or a related field.
  • Programming: Strong knowledge of Python, Java, C++, or similar languages.
  • AI/ML: Good understanding of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and NLP.
  • Projects: At least 1 AI/ML project is preferred.
  • Problem Solving: Good knowledge of Data Structures & Algorithms.
  • Experience: Freshers can apply; internships/projects are an advantage.

2.Robotics Engineer

  • Education: B.Tech/B.E./M.Tech in Robotics, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, Mechatronics, or a related field.
  • Technical Skills: Knowledge of robotics, sensors, embedded systems, electronics, and hardware.
  • Programming: Basic/strong knowledge of C/C++ and Python.
  • VR/Hardware: Understanding of VR/AR systems, microcontrollers, actuators, and hardware integration is preferred.
  • Projects: At least 1 robotics, embedded, VR, or hardware project is preferred.
  • Experience: Freshers can apply; practical project experience is an advantage.
