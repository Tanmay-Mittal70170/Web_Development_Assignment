Competitions

Explore the diverse list of competitions organized under three primary event tracks:

Registration Process

Follow these sequential steps to successfully register for events at Fest 2026:

  1. Visit the official online registration portal.
  2. Create a participant account using your valid college email address.
  3. Select your preferred competition categories and individual/team events.
  4. Upload a scanned copy of your official college student ID card.
  5. Complete the entry fee payment online via credit card, debit card, or UPI.
  6. Download and save your generated registration pass containing your unique QR code.

Rules

Please review all guidelines and mandatory requirements before entering the fest venue:

  • Carrying a valid official College Student ID Card at all times is strictly mandatory for campus entry.
  • Possession or consumption of alcoholic beverages, tobacco products, and illegal substances is strictly prohibited on campus grounds.
  • All participants must strictly report to event venues at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time.
  • Decisions made by official competition judges and fest coordinators are final and binding.
  • Damaging university property or infrastructure will lead to immediate disqualification and legal liability.

Fest Terminology Defined

Open Event
A competition open to students from all academic streams and departments without prerequisite qualifications.
Team Event
A contest requiring group participation, with fixed minimum and maximum team member thresholds.
Individual Event
A solo competition evaluating the skill and performance of a single registered participant.
Registration
The official enrollment process verifying participant eligibility and securing event slots upon fee submission.
Coordinator
A designated faculty member or student head responsible for organizing, managing, and overseeing specific events.
Final Round
The concluding stage of a competition featuring top qualifying candidates competing for podium awards.
