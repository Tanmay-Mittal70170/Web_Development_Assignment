Admission Overview

Global Apex University offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs. All prospective students are advised to read the eligibility criteria and submission guidelines thoroughly before applying.

The deadline for submitting online applications is July 31, 2026.

Eligibility

Candidates applying for degree programs must meet the baseline academic qualification requirements established by the university Senate.

Passing the qualifying examination with a minimum aggregate score of 60% is a mandatory requirement for all undergraduate courses.

Application Process

Applicants must complete the steps below in sequence:

  1. Register an account on the official university admissions portal.
  2. Fill out the online application form with accurate personal and academic details.
  3. Upload all necessary scanned certificates and supporting documents.
  4. Pay the required non-refundable application processing fee online.
  5. Submit the completed form and print the confirmation page for future reference.

Required Documents

Please ensure you have soft copies of the following items ready for upload:

  • Secondary and Higher Secondary Marksheets/Certificates
  • Government-issued Photo Identity Proof (Mandatory requirement)
  • Recent Passport-sized Photographs
  • Transfer / Migration Certificate from previous institution
  • Signed Character Certificate (Mandatory requirement)
  • Category / Caste Certificate (if applicable)

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum qualification criteria and prerequisite marks required for a candidate to apply for a program.
Merit List
A rank list of selected candidates prepared based on academic performance and entrance test scores.
Counselling
An interactive seat allocation session where document verification and course assignment take place.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to deserving students based on academic excellence or economic need.
Registration
The formal procedure of enrolling into assigned courses at the start of an academic term after fee payment.
