Academic Rules

Academic Terms Defined

Lecture
An instructional classroom session focused on theoretical principles, mathematical foundations, and core domain knowledge.
Lab
A practical computer session held in specialized laboratories for hands-on programming and system configuration.
Tutorial
An interactive session designed for small-group discussion, homework review, and clarifying complex lecture topics.
Internal Assessment
Continuous evaluation calculated through mid-semester tests, quizzes, lab performance, and regular assignments.
Practical
A formal examination testing a student's hands-on implementation capabilities and software development execution.

Sequence Before a Practical Class

  1. Store personal bags and outerwear in designated storage lockers outside the laboratory.
  2. Sign entry details and arrival timestamp into the lab attendance register.
  3. Review the lab manual and experiment instructions for the day's practical assignment.
  4. Log into your assigned terminal using your individual student portal credentials.
  5. Wait for the lab instructor's initial safety and setup briefing before running any code or simulation.

Classroom Rules

  • A minimum of 75% attendance in both lectures and practical labs is strictly mandatory to sit for end-semester examinations.
  • Mobile phones must remain switched off or placed in silent mode during all instructional sessions.
  • Food, open water containers, and beverages are strictly prohibited inside computing laboratories.
  • Students must treat lab hardware, peripherals, and network infrastructure with care and respect.
  • Plagiarism in source code, project reports, or lab journals will lead to immediate disciplinary action.
