Starters

  • Crispy Truffle Fries - Golden fried potatoes tossed in truffle oil and parmesan cheese. 20% off during Happy Hours!
  • Bruschetta Trio - Toasted baguette slices topped with marinated vine tomatoes, basil, and balsamic glaze.

Main Course

  • Pan-Seared Salmon - Wild-caught salmon fillet served with asparagus and lemon-butter herb sauce.
  • Grilled Ribeye Steak - Prime 10oz cut served with garlic mashed potatoes and red wine jus.
  • Wild Mushroom Risotto - Creamy Arborio rice with sautéed forest mushrooms and white truffle oil.

Desserts

  • Molten Chocolate Lava Cake - Warm Belgian chocolate cake with a melting center, served with vanilla bean gelato.
  • Classic Tiramisu - Traditional Italian espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with mascarpone cream. Free coffee shot with every order!

Beverages

  • Signature Berry Sparkler - A refreshing mix of wild berries, mint leaves, lime, and sparkling soda.
  • Iced Matcha Latte - Premium grade Japanese green tea whisked with almond milk and honey.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

  1. Start your journey with our crispy Bruschetta Trio paired with a light sparkling beverage.
  2. Cleanse your palate with our house-special garden green salad.
  3. Indulge in our signature Pan-Seared Salmon served hot for your main course.
  4. Conclude your evening with a slice of classic Tiramisu and a warm espresso.
