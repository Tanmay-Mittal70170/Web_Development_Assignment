Stage Descriptions

Screening
Initial review of candidate resumes and portfolio alignment.
Technical Round
Evaluation of core technical, coding, and system design skills.
HR Round
Discussion regarding company fitment, culture, compensation, and policy details.
Offer
Extending formal offer letter and onboarding guidelines.
