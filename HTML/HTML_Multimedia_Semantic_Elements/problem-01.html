Key Admission Definitions & Step-by-Step Guidelines

Important Terms Defined

Eligibility
The minimum educational qualifications, percentage thresholds, and subject prerequisites mandatory for applying to a specific degree program.
Scholarship
Financial grants and fee concessions awarded to students based on outstanding academic merit, sports achievements, or financial need.
Merit List
An official rank list generated by the university evaluating qualifying exam marks and entrance test scores to assign enrollment slots.
Application Fee
A non-refundable processing fee paid during the online submission of the university enrollment form.

Step-by-Step Admission Process

  1. Create an account on the Apex University Digital Portal using a valid email address.
  2. Select your desired degree program, qualification track, and preferred study mode.
  3. Fill in your personal, academic, and contact details accurately in the online application form.
  4. Upload scanned copies of official academic transcripts, certificates, and ID proof.
  5. Pay the non-refundable application fee using online banking, UPI, or credit card.
  6. Submit your application, download the reference receipt, and await the publication of the official merit list.
