Travel Articles & Advice

Essential Travel Tips -

Always carry sunscreen, wear comfortable cotton clothes, rent a scooter for cheap local travel, and keep cash handy for beach shacks.

Visitor Information -

Churches and historical sites usually remain open from 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM. Emergency medical & tourist helpline is available at 112.

Visitor Services & Guidelines (Description List)

Check-in
Standard resort check-in starts at 01:00 PM. Early check-in can be requested upon booking.
Guided Tour
Certified English and Hindi speaking local guides available for heritage and spice plantation tours.
Local Transport
Self-drive cars, rental scooters, and prepaid taxis are widely accessible across North & South Goa.
Travel Pass
Kadamba Transport Corporation offers day-passes for comfortable AC electric bus travel.
