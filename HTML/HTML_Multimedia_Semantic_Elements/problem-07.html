Property Financial Terms & Key Specifications

Carpet Area
Net usable floor area of the apartment excluding external walls, service shafts, and balcony space (ranging from 850 sq. ft. to 2,800 sq. ft.).
Maintenance
Estimated monthly facility maintenance charge calculated at $0.15 per sq. ft. for common area upkeep and security services.
Possession

Booking Amount
Initial token payment of 10% required to reserve unit, with customized flexi-payment plans available.
