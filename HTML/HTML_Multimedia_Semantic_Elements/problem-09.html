Tournament Terminology Defined

Match
A single organized contest or game between two individual athletes or opposing teams within a tournament framework.
Fixture
A scheduled sports event detailing the designated time, venue, pitch location, and competing opponents.
League
A round-robin tournament structure where every team plays against other participants to accumulate standings points over time.
Knockout
A single-elimination tournament model where the loser of each match is immediately eliminated from further competition.
