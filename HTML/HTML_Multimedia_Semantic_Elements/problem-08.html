Essential Financial Tips for Long-Term Wealth

  • Follow the 50/30/20 budgeting framework: 50% Needs, 30% Wants, and 20% Savings/Debt Payment.
  • Automate monthly contributions to retirement accounts and emergency savings funds.
  • Pay credit card billing balances in full every month to avoid revolving high-interest charges.
  • Monitor your credit report at least once annually to guard against unauthorized accounts.
  • Diversify your investment portfolio across stocks, bonds, real estate, and low-cost index funds.
  • Avoid impulse purchases by following the 48-hour cooling-off rule before non-essential spending.

Essential Banking & Loan Terminology Defined

Interest Rate
The annual percentage charged by a lender for borrowing money, or paid by a bank to a depositor on saved capital (expressed as an annual percentage rate APR or yield APY).
EMI (Equated Monthly Installment)
A fixed monthly payment amount made by a borrower to a lender on a specified date each calendar month, combining both principal repayment and interest charges.
Principal
The original sum of money borrowed in a loan or invested in a account, exclusive of any accumulated interest or separate finance service fees.
Tenure
The designated total duration or time window agreed upon between the borrower and lender over which a loan must be completely repaid in full.

Step-by-Step Account Opening Process

  1. Gather government-issued identification proof (Passport, Driver's License) and Proof of Address.
  2. Compare eligible financial products in our comparison table and select the ideal solution.
  3. Complete the secure digital online application or visit your nearest physical branch.
  4. Verify your identity through digital Know-Your-Customer (KYC) video or document verification.
  5. Fund your new account using an initial electronic transfer, debit card payment, or check deposit.
  6. Set up digital mobile banking credentials and configure personal fraud safety alerts.
