GLA University Greater Noida

Welcome to GLA University Greater Noida Campus. It is located at Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The campus focuses on quality education, practical learning and industry-oriented skills.

About

GLA University provides students with a modern learning environment and opportunities for academic and personal development. The university encourages practical knowledge, innovation and career development.

Programs

GLA Greater Noida offers programmes such as B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE-AIML, B.Tech Electronics and Computer Engineering, AI-Enabled BBA, AI-Enabled MBA and MCA-AIML.

Students can choose a programme according to their interests, academic goals and career plans.

Campus Life

Campus life provides students with opportunities to participate in academic activities, sports, cultural events and student activities. Students can develop teamwork, leadership and communication skills.
