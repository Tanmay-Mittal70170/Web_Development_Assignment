TECHFEST 2026

The Biggest College Technology Festival of 2026!

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a fictional college technology festival designed for students who are passionate about technology, innovation, programming and creativity. The event brings together students, developers, technology enthusiasts and industry experts under one platform.

Participants will get an opportunity to demonstrate their technical skills, participate in exciting competitions, attend informative sessions and interact with experienced professionals.

Highlights

TechFest 2026 will feature coding competitions, robotics demonstrations, web development challenges, quiz contests and innovative project exhibitions.

Students can participate in Hackathons, Coding Battles, AI Challenges and Project Exhibitions to showcase their talent and compete with other participants.

Special Announcement: Exciting prizes, certificates and internship opportunities will be available for outstanding participants.

Date & Venue

Date: 15 December 2026
Time: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Venue: GLA University, Greater Noida Campus
Location: Main Auditorium and Technology Block

The festival will begin with an opening ceremony followed by technical competitions, workshops and project demonstrations.

Speakers

TechFest 2026 will invite experienced technology professionals, entrepreneurs, software developers and educators to share their knowledge with students.

The speakers will discuss Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, Web Development, Cloud Computing and Future Technologies.

Students will also have the opportunity to ask questions and learn about careers in the technology industry.
