1. About GLA University

GLA University is a leading educational institution committed to providing quality education and creating opportunities for students to develop their academic, professional and personal skills.

The university focuses on academic excellence, innovation, research and overall personality development. Students are encouraged to participate in academic activities, technical events, cultural programs, sports and other extracurricular activities.

GLA University provides a learning environment where students can gain theoretical knowledge as well as practical experience. Innovation and skill development are important parts of the educational experience.

3. Academic Programs

Major areas of study include:

Engineering and Technology
Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication, Mechanical Engineering and other technical disciplines.

Management

Pharmacy and Applied Sciences

Education and Other Programs
The university also provides programs in education, computer applications and other professional areas.

4. Campus Facilities

GLA University provides facilities designed to support academic learning, practical training, research and student development.

Important facilities include modern classrooms, laboratories, library, computer centers, sports facilities, hostels, cafeteria and medical facilities.

The library provides students with access to books, journals, academic resources and digital learning materials. Laboratories provide practical exposure that helps students understand concepts beyond classroom learning.
