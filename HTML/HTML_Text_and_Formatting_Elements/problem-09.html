Student Terms & Rules

Important Notice for All Students

This document contains the important rules, responsibilities, and expectations that every student must follow.

General Rules

1. Students must attend all scheduled classes regularly and arrive on time.

2. Students must maintain respectful behavior toward teachers, staff, and fellow students.

3. Students must bring all required books, notebooks, and learning materials. Being prepared helps students learn effectively.

4. Mobile phones and electronic devices must only be used when permitted by the teacher.

5. Students must keep classrooms clean and must not damage institutional property.

6. Students must follow all instructions given by teachers and authorized staff.

Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75%.

Students should arrive at least 5 minutes before the scheduled class time.
Late arrival may be recorded as a late entry.

Students who are absent for an important reason should inform the appropriate teacher as soon as possible.

Regular attendance is important because classroom activities and explanations may not be repeated.

Students below the required attendance percentage may face academic restrictions.

Examination Rules

Students must arrive at the examination room before the examination begins.

Students must bring their valid student identification and required examination materials.

Mobile phones, smartwatches, unauthorized notes, and prohibited materials are not allowed.

Cheating or attempting to cheat is strictly prohibited.

Penalty: Students found cheating may receive zero marks for the examination.

Students must follow all instructions provided by the examination supervisor.

These rules ensure fairness and equal opportunity for every student.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted before the specified deadline.

The standard deadline is 11:59 PM on the assigned due date.

Example: An assignment due on Friday must be submitted before 11:59 PM Friday.

Students must submit their own original work.

Submitted files should contain the student's name, roll number, assignment title, and other required information.

Late Submission Penalty: Late assignments may receive a 10% deduction.

Assignments more than 3 days late may not be accepted unless permission is granted.

Students should contact the teacher before the deadline if they have a genuine problem.

Classroom Conduct

Students must behave respectfully toward teachers, classmates, visitors, and staff.

Students should listen when another person is speaking and avoid unnecessary interruptions.

Running, shouting, fighting, or disturbing other students is prohibited.

Intentional damage to institutional property may result in a penalty.

A positive classroom environment helps everyone learn effectively.

Discipline

Every student must follow institutional rules and maintain proper discipline.

Bullying, harassment, threats, physical violence, and intentional property damage are strictly prohibited.

Students must not use another student's account, password, identity, or academic work without permission.

Repeated violations may result in disciplinary action.

Disciplinary action depends on the seriousness and frequency of the violation.

Academic Honesty

Students must complete examinations, assignments, projects, and practical work honestly.

Plagiarism, cheating, copying, fabrication, and unauthorized collaboration are not permitted.

Students must give appropriate credit when using information or ideas from other sources.

Academic dishonesty may result in loss of marks or disciplinary action.

Academic honesty builds knowledge, responsibility, and professional behavior.

Important Deadlines

Assignment Submission: 11:59 PM on the assigned due date

Minimum Attendance: 60%

Late Assignment: 10% deduction may apply

Examination: Arrive before the examination begins.

Final Notice

All students must read, understand, and follow these terms and rules.

Failure to follow these rules may result in academic or disciplinary consequences.

These rules create a safe, fair, respectful, and productive learning environment.

Study Honestly. Attend Regularly. Submit On Time. Respect Everyone.
