What Is Photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria use sunlight to turn carbon dioxide and water into food and oxygen.

1. Sunlight: Plants capture light energy using a green pigment called chlorophyll inside their cells.

2. Inputs: Plants take in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil.

3. Outputs: The sun's energy converts these ingredients into glucose (sugar for energy) and releases oxygen into the air as a waste product.

Equation Of Photosynthesis: 6CO2+6H2O--- C6H12O6+6O2
