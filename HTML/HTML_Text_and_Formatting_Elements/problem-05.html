CHEMISTRY:


Chemistry is the scientific study of matter, its properties, composition, structure, and the changes it undergoes during chemical reactions.

Here are some chemical Formulas:

1. Water is represented as H2O

2. carbondioxide is represented as CO2

3. Sulphuric acid is represented as H2SO4

4. Copper Sulphate is represented as CuSO4

5. Barium Sulphate is represented as Ba2SO4

PHYSICS:

he scientific study of natural forces such as light, sound, heat, electricity, pressure, etc.

1. Distance between two points= √(x2-x1)2+(y2-y1)2

2. Midpoint of line segment= √[½(x1+x2)+½(y1+y2)+½(z1+z2)]

3. Volume of cylinder= πr2h

4. Surface area of Sphere = 4πr2

5. Volume of sphere= 4/3πr3h
