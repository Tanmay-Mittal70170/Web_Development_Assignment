Major Technology Center Opens in New Delhi

New facility expected to create thousands of opportunities

New Delhi has opened a new technology center designed to support innovation, education, and employment. The facility was officially opened on August 25, 2026 and is expected to become an important destination for technology professionals and students.

According to officials, the center will provide modern workspaces, training programs, and facilities for technology companies. More than 5,000 students and professionals are expected to benefit from the center every year.

The new facility will also organize workshops and educational programs covering software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and digital business. Officials said the center will help young people develop skills for future careers.

Local businesses have welcomed the development, saying it could encourage new companies and create additional employment opportunities. The center is expected to begin its first major training program in September.

City Announces New Public Learning Program

Free educational sessions to begin next month

The city administration has announced a new public learning program that will provide free educational sessions for students and young professionals.

The program will begin on September 1, 2026 and will include sessions on communication, computer skills, career planning, and basic financial awareness.

More than 2,000 participants are expected to join the first phase of the program. Registration will remain open until all available seats are filled.

Officials said the program is intended to make useful educational resources more accessible to the public. Participants will not have to pay any registration fee.
